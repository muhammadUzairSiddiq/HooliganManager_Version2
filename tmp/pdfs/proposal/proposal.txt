data.
Away Trips & Campaign
Five-level away-trip campaign with scaling rival encounters.
Recruitment centers, capture points, rewards and mission

Real-Time Strategy Gameplay
Touch selection, movement, attack and retreat commands.
NavMesh movement, rival gang AI, camera controls and

Police Pressure System

CORE SCOPE
Phase 2 - Full Feature List
The scope below is the recommended Phase 2 delivery. It is intentionally focused on the systems that most clearly move the
current build toward the agreed commercial RTS vision.
1. Landscape UI & Strategy Presentation
Landscape-first gameplay from the opening screen
onward.
Responsive HUD for common Android and iPhone aspect
ratios.
Enhanced gameplay, base, squad, recruitment and
trip-planning screens.
Improved minimap, objective display, police heat and
resource visibility.
Better touch selection, camera pan/zoom and command
feedback.
2. Home Territory & Base
A dedicated home district with headquarters as the
management hub.
Stadium surroundings, pub/social area, recruitment point,
training and recovery locations.
Squad management, healing/revive, selected upgrades
and away-trip launch from the base.
Home territory defense/capture activity linked to
progression.
3. City & RTS Gameplay Upgrade
Optimized low-poly/stylized 3D city built for mobile
performance.
Pedestrians, basic traffic, police patrols and selected
ambient transport activity.
Group movement plus move, attack, capture and retreat
actions.
Clear territory markers, rival zones and improved AI
navigation/targeting.
Home and away objectives tied to money, reputation and
police heat.
4. Playable Police Mode
A focused police gameplay mode rather than NPC-only
police.
Control a small officer squad using the same RTS
command language.
Patrol, respond to disorder, secure an area and arrest
targeted firm members.
Dedicated objective/result flow connected to the existing
police systems.
Recommended Production Boundaries
One strong home-territory/city experience is prioritized over multiple large new open-world maps in this phase.
Visual direction targets polished low-poly mobile production quality rather than high-end console realism.
Advanced police campaign progression, online multiplayer and large-scale simulation remain future expansion items.

COMMERCIAL DELIVERY
Android, iOS, Monetization & Cloud
Both mobile platforms are included in the Phase 2 technical direction. Store-specific services are integrated separately while the
gameplay codebase remains shared.
 Android / Google Play
Android landscape build and device testing.
Google Play Billing integration for agreed in-app purchase
products.
Android ad and rewarded-ad SDK integration.
Release APK/AAB preparation for Google Play
submission.
iOS / Apple App Store
iOS landscape build and iPhone safe-area validation.
Apple App Store in-app purchase integration through the
shared Unity IAP flow.
iOS ad and rewarded-ad SDK integration.
Xcode/TestFlight/App Store submission-ready build
preparation.
Monetization
In-app purchases for agreed commercial items/currency
packs.
Rewarded ads for optional player rewards at agreed
gameplay points.
Standard ad placement at non-disruptive transitions where
approved.
Purchase restore / entitlement handling required by the
target stores.
Monetization UI integrated into the landscape interface.
PlayFab Backend / Game Services
Player profile and cloud progression data.
Cloud save/sync for key player and firm progress.
Remote configuration/catalog data for economy and game
tuning.
Lightweight backend logic for agreed data validation and
game-service needs.
Multiplayer dedicated-server hosting is not part of this
Phase 2 scope.
Publishing & Account Assumptions
The client provides active Google Play Console and Apple Developer Program accounts, including required business, tax, banking
and legal information.
Client-owned store accounts, certificates/keys and required access must be available when publishing integration begins.
Google/Apple account fees, taxes and store charges are paid directly by the client. If account creation, ownership or extended
publishing management is requested from the development side, it is handled separately and may carry additional charges.
Store approval timelines are controlled by Google and Apple; the development deliverable is a tested, submission-ready build and
integration.

DELIVERY PLAN
Milestones 1-2
Each milestone ends with a reviewable build or demonstrable integration. The next milestone proceeds after the delivered
scope is reviewed and aligned.
MILESTONE 1
LANDSCAPE + CROSS-PLATFORM FOUNDATION  |  WEEKS 1-2
Convert gameplay flow and core scenes to landscape-first presentation.
Rebuild the main gameplay HUD for landscape: resources, heat, objectives, squad selection, commands and minimap.
Adapt touch camera, unit selection and RTS controls to the wider layout.
Prepare shared Android/iOS project configuration and platform-safe UI areas.
Initial Android and iOS device/test builds for layout and control review.
DELIVERABLE: PLAYABLE BUILD
MILESTONE 2
HOME TERRITORY + RTS GAMEPLAY  |  WEEKS 3-4
Implement the home district with headquarters, stadium surroundings, pub/social area, recruitment, training and recovery
locations.
Connect base management with squad preparation, healing/revive, selected upgrades and away-trip launch.
Add home territory defense/capture gameplay and clearer territory-control presentation.
Add optimized city activity: pedestrians, basic vehicle traffic, police patrol presence and selected ambient transport elements.
Improve AI navigation, group movement, objective markers and RTS mission balancing.
DELIVERABLE: PLAYABLE BUILD
Milestone Review Standard
The milestone build is reviewed against the written checklist above. Any agreed correction inside that milestone is addressed
before the project advances, keeping progress visible and reducing scope misunderstandings.
